ABOUT ME

Pallavi Sahu

BIOTECHNOLOGY STUDENT

I'm currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Biotechnology at DAVV, Indore, with a growing interest in Molecular & Computational biology, RDT, and Biological data analysis.

My interest in biotechnology started with something surprisingly small: DNA. I've always found it fascinating how a tiny molecular change can have such a huge effect on an organism. The idea of understanding these changes and eventually designing treatments around the specific needs of a patient is what drew me toward biotechnology.

I originally wanted to become a doctor, but not qualifying it, I started looking at biotechnology differently. I realised there were other ways to work toward understanding disease, developing treatments, and improving people's lives.

Over time, my interests moved toward Molecular biology, Drug design, and Computational biology. The idea of using 3D models, sequence analysis, genomics, and biological datasets to explore problems that may be difficult to approach experimentally really caught my attention.

My biological strengths include Molecular biology, Cell biology, Immunology, Biochemistry, and RDT. I've had practical exposure to PCR, DNA/RNA isolation and quantification, SDS-PAGE, Gel electrophoresis, ELISA, Blotting techniques through university training, workshops, and internships.

The computational side of my profile is still under construction. I've had introductory exposure to FASTA, BLAST, and Clustal through an NPTEL-SWAYAM course, and I'm now working toward learning Bioinformatics, Biological data analysis, Genomics, Sequence analysis, and ML applications in biology.

I'm naturally curious and like figuring things out both independently and with a team. Outside academics, I enjoy discovering new music, indie artists, and falling into completely unnecessary internet rabbit holes. I'm still early in the journey, but I have a better idea of where I want to go.

I'd love to connect with people who share a similar curiosity for biology, technology, research, or simply figuring out how things work. Always happy to exchange ideas, learn from others, and explore interesting internet rabbit holes together. :)
